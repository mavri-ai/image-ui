# Demo image notes

The images in this directory are illustrative sample content for the showcase.
They are separate from the MIT-licensed component code, are not included in the
component registry, and carry no license grant under this project's MIT license.
Use your own appropriately licensed images when reusing the examples.

Image filenames describe their role in the demos. The showcase simulates image
generation and does not make provider requests.
